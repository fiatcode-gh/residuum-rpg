# Plan — M3B `m3-balance`: the rebalance

Branch `m3-balance`, base `b9d7c21`. Spec and recon live under the local
ledger directory and are cited by absolute path in the build report.

Baseline measured on unmodified code before any change: core 529 +
content 396 + app 371 = 1296 green. Survivability: crypt
`24/40 (60.0%), 1:1 2:9 3:6 5:24`, fleetfoot 7/40, sea-cave
`34/40 (85.0%)`, keep `31/40 (77.5%)`.

## Order of work

Each step is one commit whose exit state is green. Test-driven per unit
of behaviour: the failing test first, the smallest change that passes it,
then the tidy. Every re-pinned figure lands in the same commit as the
change that moved it, with the old value quoted in the commit message.

The tuning steps come last on purpose. Everything before them changes
what the balance bot meets, so a band re-pinned earlier would have to be
re-pinned again.

### 1. Discovery stops being a reward for walking

`arrivingAt` unions in only the node arrived at. The adjacency
assertions in `whereabouts_test.dart` are rewritten red-first and the old
assertion is quoted in the commit. One test per layer for the
undiscovered definition, so the app's `rumorOnOffer` and core's `_untold`
cannot drift apart. The rumor purchase gains a second composed log line
naming the place.

### 2. Camp expiry

A new hero-level `campDay` key on `SavedHero`, written at suspend from
`world.day`, cleared on resume, completion, abandon and death. Codec,
decoder and the two refusal rows land with it; goldens are re-written by
hand in the same commit. Overrun at `world.day - campDay >= 3`, warned at
2, checked at the fork and at the resume attempt.

### 3. A delve completes

Leaving from the bottom floor ends the run alive behind a confirm.
`_onRunEnded` adopts suspend's conditional merchant keep. The world
fork's resume label gains the delve's size.

### 4. Beats

Three composed app-level lines: the bottom-of-delve arrival, the
`boss-` kill, and the completion confirm. No core event, no core
machinery.

### 5. The road scales, and the new roads get their own creatures

`dangerOn(route, profile)` in content; `travelOneDay` takes the danger as
a value. `roadSpawnTable` becomes a `DungeonSpawnTable` so a themed table
can hold creatures the global bestiary does not list; `roadTableFor`
picks lowland, shore or garrison.

### 6. Disabled controls say why

`ItemRow.reason`, rendered only while the row is disabled. Merchant buy
and buy-back, the world Walk row, the inn's gold half, and the bank's
gold buttons.

### 7. The designed-difficulty pin

A depth-scoped content test: every creature in any spawn table at depth
three or deeper must beat the survivability kit's armour by at least two
with its best blow. The armour is derived from the kit's own loadout
rather than written down. The shallow nuisances are named exemptions.

### 8. Tuning, measured

Litter cut, drop chances, merchant gear count, then pierce and the deep
attack ceilings. The bands are re-measured after every delta and the
whole trail is kept. If a target cannot be reached inside the first-guess
deltas the architect is told with the trail, and nothing is invented.

### 9. The xp dartdoc says forty

Prose only. The curve is untouched.

## What must not move

`step.dart`, `generator.dart`, `game_state.dart`, `run_boundary.dart`,
`loadout.dart`, `event.dart`, `dungeons.dart`, `dungeon_spawn.dart`,
`armory.dart`, `affix_pool.dart`, `new_game.dart`. No hero formula, kit,
price or layout-generator change. No save-version bump. No new core
event. The crypt's floor layouts stay byte-identical.

Ruling 8 — the equip clamp unification and the cap-overflow fix — needs
`step.dart` and `wear.dart`, both of which are outside that fence. It is
held until the architect rules, and it lands before the tuning if it
lands at all.
