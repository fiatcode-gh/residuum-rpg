# Proposed Unit 13 Contract — Visual Parity Re-baseline

## Status

- WHAT: **approved by the user**
- Unit type: **architect/design audit**
- Production implementation: **none**
- Authorization: **not carried**

## Outcome

The remaining Visual Reboot work is no longer guided by the old subsystem sequence. The project leaves this unit with an explicit ten-frame parity inventory, a settled production visual-language contract, a settled authored-asset strategy, and a recut roadmap whose future units each close named gaps against named approved mock frames.

## Scope

Audit all approved mock frames:

1. Stonebridge
2. Dungeon exploration
3. Combat
4. Targeting
5. Expanded combat log
6. Character
7. Spells
8. Pack
9. Forge
10. Tavern

For each frame record:

- composition/layout;
- typography;
- density;
- surfaces/framing;
- palette/value hierarchy;
- iconography;
- illustration/art;
- interaction-state presentation;
- dungeon/world structural rendering where applicable;
- missing asset vocabulary.

Every significant discrepancy is classified as:

- `CODE`
- `ASSET`
- `CODE + ASSET`
- `INTENTIONAL DEVIATION`
- `ALREADY ACCEPTABLE`

Settle:

- typography roles and font-asset policy;
- color/accessibility policy;
- frame/surface/ornament vocabulary;
- spacing/density principles;
- control-state grammar;
- authored-asset authority;
- composition freedom vs behavioral locks.

Then recut the remaining visual-reboot roadmap from the audit.

## Non-goals

- no production UI changes;
- no renderer changes;
- no new production assets;
- no gameplay/content/balance changes;
- no save/schema changes;
- no map-bleed patch;
- no post-death save debugging;
- no execution-grade implementation plan for future units before their WHAT is approved.

## Decisions to reopen

Earlier presentation decisions may be superseded where parity evidence shows they block the approved target, including:

- monospace-only visual identity;
- overly conservative use of color;
- large Material-derived card geometry;
- treating current compositions as nearly fixed;
- postponing authored asset expansion.

## Behavioral/accessibility locks

Do not reopen merely for parity:

- engine authority/determinism;
- authoritative gameplay vocabulary;
- melee map-first;
- targeted spell arm → target → tap;
- map/timeline/log/action-region responsibilities;
- activation repetition/secrecy;
- selection-vs-targeting semantic distinction;
- no state by hue alone;
- save compatibility unless a separate approved defect unit changes it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. All ten approved frames have a current-state comparison.
2. Every significant discrepancy uses the shared gap vocabulary.
3. Every discrepancy has one ownership classification.
4. Typography strategy is settled.
5. Color/accessibility strategy is settled.
6. Surface/frame/ornament vocabulary is settled.
7. Authored-asset authority and required asset families are settled.
8. Presentation freedoms are explicitly separated from locked gameplay/accessibility contracts.
9. Unit 12.5 AC17 is superseded with an assessment supported by the full side-by-side evidence.
10. The map-bleed defect and save-read candidate each have a route without being solved here.
11. The remaining roadmap is recut from the matrix rather than preserved by old numbering.
12. Every proposed future implementation unit names its target mock frames and parity gaps.
13. No production file or production asset is changed by Unit 13.
14. The recut roadmap is presented to the user for approval before implementation planning begins.

## Evidence standard

The approved mock is the source of truth for appearance. Current screenshots/source are evidence of implementation state.

Judge parity through composition, hierarchy, density, typography, surface/material language, color/value, icon/illustration vocabulary, structural depth, interaction-state clarity, and overall cohesion.

Do not substitute “internally consistent” for “close to the approved mock.”
