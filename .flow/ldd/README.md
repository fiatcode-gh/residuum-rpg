# Ledger-driven development

`.flow/ldd/` is the tracked authority for active and completed LDD work.

- LDD epics live under `.flow/ldd/<epic>/` with their `LEDGER.md` and
  `RESUME.md`.
- Durable standalone product and design specifications live under `docs/specs/`.
- External independent-session handoffs live under an epic's `external/`
  directory.
- Untracked visual, device, and build evidence lives under `.flow/evidence/`.

Historical M1, M2, M3, and legacy artifacts remain in place. They are historical
context; an active epic's ledger and resume are the current LDD authority.
