## Goal

The world's two empty promises become real places: the sea-cave (one day from
Northgate) and the ruined keep (two days out, the hardest walk). Each has its
own bestiary, spawn and drop tables, floor stream (per-node salt), a named
bottom-floor boss guarding one guaranteed rare, and a survivability band. A
hero's run knows which dungeon it is in — on disk, across suspend, resume,
and boot. The crypt's floors, tables, and the exact 24/40 stay byte-identical,
and the 24/40 stops being prose: it becomes a code assertion.

## Scope fence (what this unit is NOT)

- No randomized or per-dungeon depth. Every dungeon is five floors;
  `deepestDepth` is untouched. That is the next unit (`m3-depth, D43).
- No new tiles, no themed terrain, no second layout generator. All three
  dungeons use `generateFloor` as-is; theme is content (creatures, tables,
  names, palette).
- No core boss machinery: no flag on `Actor, no victory event, no
  distinguished spawn slot. M4 owns that.
- No set pieces, no spell books (M4 / m3-magic); the per-dungeon drop-table
  shape this unit builds is their landing pad, nothing more.
- No camp-expiry or road-danger changes (follow-up 24 waits on the playtest).
- **Core `lib/` diff must be EMPTY.** This unit is content + app only. If a
  step seems to need a core edit, stop and pre-declare.

## Shape — precedents to follow (read them, do not reason by analogy)

- **Per-dungeon tables:** the road. `roadSpawnTable` and `roadDropTable` live
  beside the global maps, never inside them, and reach combat through
  `startRun`'s existing `dropTables` parameter
  (`packages/content/lib/src/world.dart:166-206, `:229-262`).
- **Per-node salt:** `_townSalt` (`packages/content/lib/src/economy.dart:140-147`)
  — FNV over the node id's own text, XORed into `worldSeed` before
  `floorSeed, "so a town added to the world needs no second edit to get a
  shelf". Salt-hygiene and stream-disjointness tests:
  `packages/content/test/world_test.dart:540-591`.
- **A new function beside the crypt's path, never an edit inside it:**
  `generateEncounter`'s own dartdoc (`encounter_map.dart:62-70`) is the
  argument, M3R is the precedent.
- **Save reshape:** the M3W world block — required field, goldens
  regenerated BY HAND in the same commit, refusal tests that delete the field
  from an encoded document (`world_characterization_test.dart` patterns).
- **Discovery:** the rumor pool (`world.dart:129-146, `rumor.dart:81-105`) —
  one shared ordered pool, `_firstUntold` against `discovered`.

## Design rulings (architect authority; the user may veto before launch)

1. **Dungeon identity lives at the HERO level of the save, beside `run` and
   `inside`** — a `dungeon` key whose value is the node id string, null
   exactly when `run` is null. Not inside the run block: `encodeRun` takes a
   `GameState, and `GameState` must not grow a node field (that would drag
   world identity into core and give road encounters a meaningless slot).
   `loadRun` gains a dungeon parameter and builds
   `dungeonFor(dungeon, worldSeed)(visit)` instead of the hardcoded
   `residuumDungeon`. Decode refusals: `dungeon` present-but-unknown, naming
   a non-dungeon node, null while `run` exists, or set while `run` is null —
   all `SaveMalformed, refused by name.
2. **`dungeonFor(NodeId, int worldSeed)` is the only door to a floor
   stream.** For `cryptNode` it returns `residuumDungeon(worldSeed)` — the
   crypt bypasses the salt entirely (identity by construction, not "salt of
   zero"). For the new nodes it returns builders over the new themed-floor
   function with `worldSeed ^ dungeonSalt(node)`. Unknown node throws
   `ArgumentError`.
3. **The rumor pool stays single and ordered** (northgate, crypt, sea-cave,
   ruined keep). D43's "staggered" discovery comes from pool order plus
   travel distance, not per-town pools — per-town pools are new machinery
   nothing needs yet. The crypt entry stays unsellable; the two new entries
   sell at the standing `rumorPrice`.
4. **Entering a dungeon while camped elsewhere abandons the camp behind a
   confirmation** that names the camp's dungeon. The Resume/Delve-anew fork
   appears only at the camp's own node. Any fresh entry bumps the visit; the
   D36 merchant invariant follows from the existing visit+town keying and is
   restated in the tests.
5. **The loot stream is per-dungeon for the new dungeons:** they pass
   `lootSeedSalt: lootStreamSalt ^ dungeonSalt(node)`. The crypt path passes
   exactly `lootStreamSalt, unchanged.
6. **The bot's mid-progression kit is a test fixture, not content.** First
   guess (worker measures, tunes, and reports; architect ratifies the pins):
   a fine-rarity weapon and armor from the existing armory, four potions,
   Arms/Might/Bulwark at level 5. Bands: sea-cave and keep must each land in
   0.50–0.95 with that kit, exact figures pinned once measured.
7. **Palette is additional signal only.** The painter gains a per-theme
   palette (walls/floor tint per dungeon); every state remains readable by
   glyph, word, and position; greyscale legibility holds (new shots prove
   it). The crawl app bar names the dungeon in words ("The Sea-Cave —
   depth 3/5"); encounters keep "The road".

## Per-item contracts

### 1. `dungeonSalt` and `dungeonFor` (content)

- `dungeonSalt(NodeId)`: `_townSalt`'s FNV shape over the id text, as a TWIN
  in `dungeons.dart` — `economy.dart` stays untouched. Two copies is inside
  the duplicate-twice-before-extracting rule; extraction is the third
  caller's job.
- `dungeonFor(cryptNode, s)` returns `residuumDungeon(s)` — pinned: floors
  byte-identical to `residuumDungeon`'s across a sweep of seeds, depths,
  visits.
- New-node builders: `floorSeed(worldSeed ^ dungeonSalt(node), depth, visit)`
  feeds `generateFloor` exactly as `buildFloor` does; same draw-order
  contract (count → itemCount → layout → creatures → items), documented.
- Stream disjointness: sea-cave, keep, and crypt floor seeds never collide
  across the standing sweep sizes; `dungeonSalt(seaCave), `dungeonSalt(ruinedKeep)` distinct from each other and every existing salt.

### 2. `themedFloor` and the bottom floor (content)

- Depths 1–4: exactly `buildFloor`'s shape with the dungeon's own tables.
- Depth 5: after rolling monsters, the LAST monster spawn's creature is
  replaced by the dungeon's boss (`id: 'boss-<node>'`); the floor asks
  `generateFloor` for one extra item spawn and places one trophy item there —
  rolled from the dungeon's bottom drop table with rarity forced
  rare-or-better (the weight-zero idiom: a rarities list whose sub-rare
  tiers carry weight zero), `id: 'trophy-<node>'`.
- Exactly one boss per bottom floor, zero on depths 1–4, zero in the crypt —
  each half tested.
- New characterization goldens once built (M3W C1 lesson): one full ASCII +
  spawn-list pin per new dungeon at a named seed/depth, pasted from a real
  run, never edited by tooling.

### 3. `startDungeonRunAt(NodeId, Profile)` (content)

- `startRun(profile, dungeon: dungeonFor(node, profile.worldSeed),
  dropTables: <the node's>, lootSeedSalt: <per ruling 5>)`.
- At `cryptNode` it is `startDungeonRun`'s exact successor: a test pins the
  first floor and opening rolls byte-identical to the old door before the
  old door is deleted (characterization first, then delete).
- `startDungeonRun` is removed; all callers migrate.

### 4. The save document (content)

- Hero entry gains REQUIRED key `dungeon` (string node id or null); the
  worker picks its stable spot in the written order and the goldens pin it.
- Consistency refusals per ruling 1, each refused BY NAME in the error.
- All three golden documents regenerated by hand in the same commit; the
  `world_characterization_test.dart` net gains: delete the `dungeon` key →
  refused naming it; `dungeon` set while run is null → refused; unknown id →
  refused; `dungeon` naming a town → refused.
- Pre-M3D documents (no `dungeon` key) are refused → verify-then-rotate
  fallback → fresh hero. Unshipped, sanctioned (M3W precedent). Say so in
  the report.

### 5. App: the doors and the carry

- `EnterDungeonPressed(NodeId)` / `DelveAnewPressed(NodeId)`; the world
  screen passes the node under the hero (`world_screen.dart:302` has it).
- TownBloc state carries the camp's dungeon wherever it carries `run`; every
  emission that preserves `run` preserves `dungeon` (the M3L `_settled`
  landmine class — pinned by a disk-watching mutation row).
- World-screen doors at a dungeon node: no camp → Enter; camp here → Resume /
  Delve anew (delve-anew confirmation as today); camp elsewhere → Enter with
  a confirmation naming the camp's dungeon ("This abandons your camp at The
  Crypt."). Resume never offered away from the camp's node.
- Autosaver writes the hero-level `dungeon`; boot hands it back; resume from
  boot lands in the right dungeon (the saved builder comes from
  `dungeonFor`).
- Crawl app bar: "<Dungeon name> — depth N/5"; road encounters keep "The
  road". Log line on resume may name the dungeon (worker's call).

### 6. Palette (app)

- `GlyphGrid` accepts a small per-theme palette (wall/floor/stairs tints);
  crypt keeps today's exact colors (byte-same constants). New palettes must
  pass the greyscale read: shots in greyscale where floors, walls, stairs,
  monsters, items, hero all separate by value/glyph.

### 7. Survivability (content tests)

- The crypt test's printed line becomes assertions: wins == 24, the death
  histogram map equals {1:1, 2:9, 3:6, 5:24}, fleetfoot wins == 7 — beside
  the existing band, same test, same seeds, still `newGame` at visit 0.
- New per-dungeon band tests: bot enters via `startDungeonRunAt` with the
  ruling-6 fixture profile; band 0.50–0.95 asserted, `stalled == 0, exact
  figures printed AND pinned once measured (worker pins the measured number;
  the architect re-runs and ratifies). Turn budget stays 4000 unless the
  worker shows it stalls honest play — report if touched.
- The orphan test (`content_validation_test.dart:156`) generalizes RED-FIRST
  to the union of all dungeons' + the road's tables PLUS the placed bosses
  (bosses are met, never rolled — the test's "somewhere" must know both
  doors), before the first themed bestiary lands.

## Behaviour arguments that must land in documentation

- Why the crypt bypasses the salt (identity by construction; the printed and
  now asserted 24/40 is the proof) — on `dungeonFor`.
- Why identity is hero-level, not run-block or GameState (core stays
  world-agnostic in the crawl; encounters have no node) — on the codec.
- Why bosses are placed, never rolled (a weighted boss is either a lie at
  weight 1000 or a lottery at less; the bottom floor is a promise) — on the
  placement.
- Why the trophy rides an extra item spawn (raising itemCount cannot
  reshuffle layout or monsters — `generator.dart:100-102`'s own promise) —
  on the trophy.
- Why the rumor pool stays single and ordered (ruling 3) — on the pool.
- The abandon-elsewhere confirmation (one run slot per hero is a save-format
  fact, not a UI choice) — on the world-screen doors.
